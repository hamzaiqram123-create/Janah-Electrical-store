Janah Al Riyada Co. (شركة جناح الريادة) is an electrical supplies retailer in Saudi Arabia. This app is its
shop: customers browse switches, sockets, lighting, cables and breakers, order for delivery anywhere in the
Kingdom (or pick up from the shop), track orders, download VAT tax invoices and request returns.

Native features of the app:
- Native launch, loading, offline and error screens in Arabic, English and Urdu, following the device language.
- Tax invoices print through the iOS print sheet (print or save as PDF).
- Exports and attachments are saved through the share sheet (Files, AirDrop, Mail).
- Phone, e-mail, WhatsApp and map links open in their own apps; other websites open in an in-app Safari view.
- Swipe from the edge to go back, pull to refresh, Dynamic Type in the native screens.

Account: browsing and guest checkout work without an account. The demo account above shows the order history,
invoices and saved addresses. Account deletion: Account → Delete account (asks for the password).

Payments: only physical goods are sold. Customers pay cash on delivery, or by card on the payment provider's
page when online payment is enabled. No digital goods or subscriptions.
